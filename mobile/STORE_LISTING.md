## Apple App Store

**Subtitle (≤30):** Track signs & synchronicities

**Promotional text (≤170):** Set an intention. Log the signs that show up. Watch the threads between them appear, and walk a nine-stage guided journey at your own pace.

**Keywords (≤100, no spaces after commas):**
synchronicity,manifest,intention,journal,signs,angel numbers,coincidence,spiritual,vision board

**Description (≤4000):**

Synchros is a quiet place to notice when your inner world and the outer world line up.

Write down what you want to bring into your life. Then, when something happens that feels too well timed to be chance, log it in seconds: the repeated number, the stranger who said exactly what you needed, the song, the dream. Thread each sign to the intention it speaks to, and over time you’ll see the patterns for yourself.

INTENTIONS
Write each intention in your own words, add a script describing it as already real, and choose the feeling behind it. When it comes true, mark it manifested and record how it showed up.

SIGNS
One tap from anywhere. Choose what kind of sign it was, how strongly it landed, and which intention it belongs to.

THREADS
Your Today screen shows your week as two circles: your intentions and the signs you’ve noticed. The more signs you thread to an intention, the closer the circles draw together.

THE JOURNEY
Nine guided stages, taken in order. Each has a short teaching, one real-world practice and three reflections, from noticing coincidences to following your intuition and living with purpose.

PATTERNS
See your signs week by week, which kinds show up most, when they tend to arrive, and how long your intentions take to manifest.

VISION BOARD
Arrange photos and words for what you’re calling in. Drag, pinch and twist them into place.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No tracking. No ads. Everything stays on your phone, and you can save a backup file whenever you like.

**App Privacy (nutrition label):** Data Not Collected.

**Export compliance:** Handled by `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption: false` in app.json.

**Review notes:** No account or login is required. All data is stored locally on the device. The optional daily reminder is a local notification.
