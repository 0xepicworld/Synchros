# Synchros privacy policy

_Effective October 4, 2026_

Synchros is made by EPICWORLD (Erik Chirkoff). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Synchros app on iPhone or Android.

## The short version

Synchros does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything you write stays on your phone.

## What the app stores, and where

- **Your journal** (intentions, signs, reflections, journey progress, settings) is stored in a database on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
- **Vision board photos** you choose are copied into the app's private storage on your device. Synchros reads only the photos you pick; it never browses your photo library.
- **Reminders** are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification service or server is involved.

## What we do not do

- No accounts, sign-ins or email collection.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

## Backups

If you use **Save a backup**, the app creates a file containing your journal and hands it to your phone's share sheet. Where that file goes (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email and so on) is your choice, and that destination's own privacy policy applies. Your phone's operating system may also include app data in its own device backups, according to your device settings.

## Deleting your data

Open **Settings → Delete all data** to permanently erase everything Synchros has stored on your phone. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

## Children

Synchros is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
